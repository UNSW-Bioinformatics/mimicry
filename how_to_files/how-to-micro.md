## How to: Exploring the Microbiome and Body Location   

The Microbiome tab in MIMICRY can be used to explore the microbial context of candidate molecular mimics. It provides a way to move from an individual microbial match to the broader question of where the matching organism may occur in the human microbiome.


### Explore microbial taxa and peptide distribution
Below the Tree of Life, the Microbiome tab provides additional views that summarise the organisms carrying microbial mimic peptides and show how widely individual peptides occur across organisms. 

1. The panels include a barplot of the ranks microbial phyla, class, genus, species or strain by the number of distinct peptides associated with that grouping. 
3. The How far each peptide spreads plot counts how many organisms carry the selected peptides. This provides insight into the shared nature of the peptides across the microbial organisms.
4. The remaining plots are heatmaps that provides taxonomic groupings by cancer/tissue, amongst others. These are exploratory, and further custom plots can be made in the "Custom Chart" section.

### Body locations 
The body locations tab extends the analysis from the Microbiome tab by including filters and heatmaps looking at the body site of the selected microbial candidates, and not just the organisms. This grouping allows further insight into the locations of the microbes, for further characterisation of the candidate mimicry peptides. 
The current data plots include the body site grouped with other fields such as cancer, tissue, or taxonomy.  

Other options to note: 
1. Barplots can be redrawn as pie charts.
2. Heatmaps can be redrawn as dot plots.
3. All plots can be exported as images or the underlying data to save and plot elsewhere.
