## How-to: Cancer & Mutation
This tab can be used to better understand the origin of the candidate mimicry-epitopes.  
This section displays visualisations describing the distribution of the different conditions collected across all the cancer datasets that had matched microbial proteins. The tab contains several interactive charts and heatmaps. Together, the panels provide different views of the same underlying mimicry data.

### Exploring mutations
The Variant type panel shows the type of genomic alteration associated with the represented peptides.

### Exploring peptides across cancers
The Peptide by cancer heatmap shows how individual peptide sequences are represented across different cancer or sample annotations.
Read across a peptide row to see the cancer contexts in which that peptide occurs.
Read down a cancer column to compare the peptides represented in that cancer context.

### Exploring HLA binding by cancer
The HLA allele by cancer heatmap connects cancer annotations with MIMICRY's HLA-binding predictions.
Read across an HLA row to compare the cancers represented for a particular allele.
Read down a cancer column to compare HLA alleles associated with strong-binding peptides in that cancer. 

### Connecting cancer to microbial mimics
The Cancer by microbial phylum or genus heatmap provides a taxonomic view. 
Rows represent cancer or sample categories. Columns represent microbial phyla or genus.
Colour shows the number of unique peptides for each cancer-taxonomy combination.

### Source gene by cancer
The Source gene by cancer heatmap connects human source genes directly with cancer or tissue contexts.
